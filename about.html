About the project

A digital desk for
getting things done.

01

Project description

Study Lock-in is a small browser-based study environment for students who want a quieter place to work. It brings a timer, ambient audio, tasks, motivational quotes and progress tracking into one focused space.

02

Purpose

The idea is deliberately simple: open the site, choose an atmosphere, set a realistic session, write down what you need to do and start. The interface avoids feeds, notifications and unnecessary screens.

03

Technologies

  • HTMLStructure and semantic content
  • CSSLayout, responsive design and themes
  • JavaScriptTimer, audio, tasks, themes and storage
  • LocalStorageRemember tasks, progress and theme
